# Introduction: Why Cross-System Synthesis Is Hard

## The problem

Temporal credit assignment asks how a learner connects a later outcome to an earlier event that should be updated. One influential family of ideas uses an **eligibility trace**: recent activity leaves a temporary record that can be modified when a later teaching or modulatory signal arrives.[1] This is a useful theory for linking fast neural events to later consequences, not a universal biological mechanism.

Invertebrate systems are valuable because they can connect behavior, identified circuits, and synaptic change. For example, *Aplysia* conditioning has been studied alongside identified synapses, while *Drosophila* research has examined reinforcement signaling in defined dopaminergic systems.[2] [3] Different organisms, tasks, measurements, and levels of explanation do not automatically produce the same quantity.

## What synthesis requires

A synthesis is not simply averaging numbers from several studies. Before results are compared, the studies need a **prospective bridge**: a stated target system, event or intervention, comparator, outcome definition, measurement scale, timing convention, and mapping from each source-specific measure to a common benchmark. Guidance for evidence synthesis emphasizes pre-specified grouping and careful assessment of whether comparisons and outcomes are sufficiently similar for quantitative combination.[4] [5]

A bridge chosen after results are known can silently privilege the measures that look most compatible or most favorable. Defining it first makes the comparison rule reviewable and helps distinguish a valid pooled analysis from a structured account of non-comparable evidence.

## What this project found

The synthesis question is **unresolved, not negative**. Current source-specific records do not share a prospectively specified bridge. They therefore cannot justify a pooled effect, a common benchmark, a ranking, cross-system translation, or a shared biological mechanism.

The contributing records also differ in readiness: one re-analysis is directionally suggestive but unstable, one structural assessment is inconclusive across reference families, and one observational encoding project contains a bounded supportive route alongside a separate non-supportive route. These records remain source-specific rather than being converted into a common score.

The repository’s timing-contrast helper performs arithmetic on caller-supplied synthetic sequences. It is an implementation reference, not an empirical effect estimate or a biological test.

## What would change the status

A future synthesis would need a bridge specified before candidate records are inspected. That bridge could support a quantitative comparison if the resulting evidence is genuinely comparable, or it could support a transparent source-specific synthesis if it is not. Either outcome would be informative.
