# Current Results and Discussion

## Current Result

The cross-system synthesis remains **unresolved**. The available records were reviewed for whether they could be compared using a shared rule established before inspection. They cannot currently be combined because no such bridge was specified. A bridge would state what is being compared, how it is measured, and how each record maps to the same benchmark.

The supportive result at this stage is methodological rather than biological: the review makes the comparison boundary clear. Each record must remain in its own context. The non-supportive result is that the present records do not support a pooled effect, a common benchmark, a ranking, cross-system translation, or a shared-mechanism conclusion.

The available records also do not point in a single stable direction. They include directionally suggestive but unstable evidence, evidence that remains inconclusive across alternative reference choices, a bounded supportive encoding observation, and a separate non-supportive observational route. These descriptions are retained as source-specific status statements, not converted into a shared result.

The timing helper is complete only as a data-free arithmetic reference. It calculates a contrast from caller-supplied synthetic sequences and checks that those inputs are valid. It is not an empirical estimate and does not test a biological hypothesis.

## Interpretation

The current result does not mean that a cross-system relationship is absent. It means that the available material does not provide a fair, pre-specified way to evaluate that relationship. Combining unlike tasks, measures, or timing conventions without such a rule could create an apparent pattern that comes from the comparison method rather than from the underlying systems.

For a reader outside the field, the key point is simple: evidence from different settings can be useful without being directly interchangeable. The current contribution is to state that limit plainly and preserve the distinctions among the records.

## What the Result Does Not Show

This work does not show a pooled effect or a common benchmark. It does not establish that any record is stronger or weaker than another, or that a result in one system translates to another. It does not identify a shared biological mechanism, demonstrate causality, or settle the broader temporal-credit-assignment question.

No external bridge material has been selected, screened, or analysed for this synthesis. No pooled analysis, ranking, cross-system translation, or shared-mechanism test has been run. The synthetic timing helper does not change any of these limits.

## Discussion and Future Testing

Future work should begin only with a prospectively specified and reviewable bridge, established before candidate records are inspected. The first testable question is whether a proposed bridge defines a common target, comparator, outcome, measurement scale, timing convention, and mapping rule clearly enough that independent reviewers would make the same inclusion and comparison decisions.

A second testable question is whether each candidate record can meet that bridge without changing its original meaning. A safeguard is to record exclusions and non-comparable cases explicitly rather than revising the bridge to accommodate them after review.

A third testable question is whether reasonable alternative bridge choices lead to the same qualitative interpretation. A safeguard is to specify the alternatives and the decision rule in advance, keep their evaluations separate, and report instability rather than selecting the most favorable framing.

A further question is whether any proposed common benchmark preserves the distinction between an arithmetic timing contrast and an empirical biological inference. A safeguard is to keep synthetic validation, empirical evidence, and interpretation in clearly separate materials and to avoid treating the helper as evidence of a mechanism.

Any research-facing evaluation requires maintainer approval and an appropriate data boundary outside this public repository. Until those conditions are met, the appropriate outcome remains a careful source-specific account rather than a combined conclusion.
