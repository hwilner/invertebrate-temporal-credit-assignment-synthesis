# Deferred and Dropped Directions

## Purpose

This document records categories of work that are intentionally absent from the public staging tree. It does not describe private research details or report outcomes.

## Dropped from this public tree

The following materials are excluded rather than adapted for public use:

- Research data, transformed datasets, and derived outputs.
- Result-bearing documentation, figure specifications, figures, and generated reports.
- Internal archive indexes, access records, and operational tracking material.
- Downloads, external source material, source-specific metadata, and local provenance files.
- Notebooks and any test that writes to repository data or output locations.

## Deferred directions

Empirical assessment, external-material collection, outcome interpretation, and figure preparation are outside the scope of this repository. If considered elsewhere, they require separate governance and must not be inferred from the data-free utility or documentation retained here.

## What remains appropriate here

Appropriate contributions are conceptual clarification, public-boundary maintenance, deterministic pure-Python utilities, and synthetic tests. These activities may improve the repository’s clarity without implying an empirical conclusion.
